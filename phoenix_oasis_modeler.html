Under the Hood: Modeling Mechanics

1. The Core Equation

Net income is calculated after pre-tax Section 125 cafeteria deductions (health/dental/vision) and Traditional 401(k) retirement contributions. The remaining cash flow is governed by:

Surplus = Net Paycheck - (Accelerated Loan + Housing & Utilities + Lifestyle Baseline)

2. Arizona & Federal Tax Assumptions

  • Federal Tax: Evaluated with standard single deduction (~$14,600 annualized) across marginal 10%, 12%, and 22% brackets.
  • Arizona State Tax: Uses Arizona's statutory flat 2.5% rate with standard deduction allowances.
  • FICA (7.65%): Evaluated against gross earnings less pre-tax health/dental (Section 125 exempt; 401(k) subject to FICA).

3. Automated 100-Point Scoring

Scenarios must meet the non-negotiable $200/mo reserve floor to be certified. Scores reward debt paydown acceleration, comfortable daily living buffers, and year-end bank resilience after absorbing planned vacation expenses.
