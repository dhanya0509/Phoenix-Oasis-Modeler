# 🌵 Phoenix Oasis Modeler

A lightweight, interactive cash flow simulator designed to help you model room rent, aggressive debt payoff, and savings goals in Phoenix, Arizona.

## 🔗 Live Demo

👉 **[Try the Live Interactive Modeler](https://dhanya0509.github.io/Phoenix-Oasis-Modeler/)**

---

## 💡 What It Does

Most budgets track past spending. This tool lets you test choices **before** signing a lease or committing to a loan payment plan:

* **Real-Time Paycheck Math:** Calculates take-home pay from a $60k salary after pre-tax benefits (401k, health, dental) and Arizona's $2.5\%$ flat tax.
* **3 Interactive Levers:** Adjust loan payments, housing/utilities, and daily lifestyle spending with live sliders.
* **Non-Negotiable $200/month Savings Floor:** Instantly flags whether a plan leaves a safe emergency cushion.
* **Vacation Planner:** Simulates a mid-year trip and shows your final 12-month bank balance.
* **Scenario Comparison:** Ranks your plan against 4 common Phoenix living archetypes (from suburban commuting to downtown studios).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
* **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)
* **Charts:** [Chart.js](https://www.chartjs.org/)
* **Zero Dependencies:** Runs directly in any modern browser without npm or build steps.
